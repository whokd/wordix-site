Wordix privacy policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Overview

Wordix is a word game and vocabulary practice app. The app does not require an account and is designed to keep gameplay data on the user's device.

Information we collect

Wordix does not ask users to create an account. Gameplay progress, settings, scores, and saved words are stored locally on the device so the app can remember the user's experience. This local app data is not sent to Wordix servers.

If a user contacts Wordix support by email, we receive the user's email address and any information the user chooses to include in the message, such as device details, screenshots, app version, or a description of the issue.

How information is used

Local app data is used to run the game, show saved word cards, keep settings, and display scores. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Support emails are used only to respond to questions, troubleshoot issues, and improve Wordix based on user feedback.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Wordix does not use third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics services. Wordix does not sell personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the user's device until the user changes it in the app or deletes the app. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond to the request and maintain support records.

Children's privacy

Wordix is not directed to children under 9. The app does not include accounts, forms, chat, profiles, or any other in-app way for users to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when Wordix changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact wordix@proton.me .
