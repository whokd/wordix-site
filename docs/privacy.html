Wordix privacy policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Overview

Wordix is a word game and vocabulary practice app. This draft policy is intended for an app that stores gameplay progress and saved word cards locally on the user's device and does not require an account.

Information we collect

Wordix does not ask users to create an account. Gameplay progress, settings, scores, and saved words are stored on the device so the app can remember the user's experience.

How information is used

Local app data is used to run the game, show saved word cards, keep settings, and display scores. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.
