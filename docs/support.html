Common questions

  • To save a word pair, finish a round and tap the star on a card you want to keep.
  • To review saved words, open Learn and choose the language pair you want to practice.
  • To remove a saved word, open Learn and tap the star on that saved card.
  • If a word is not accepted, it may not be included in the bundled dictionary data.

Dictionary data

Wordix uses dictionary data derived from FreeDict. FreeDict is an independent dictionary project, and Wordix does not author or verify every word, translation, or entry included in the source data.

For missing words, unexpected translations, or dictionary-content complaints, please refer to the FreeDict project. For app bugs, layout issues, crashes, or gameplay feedback, contact Wordix support.

Feature requests and feedback

Wordix is built around short word-chain sessions and saved vocabulary practice. Suggestions for better wording, language coverage, or gameplay balance are welcome.
